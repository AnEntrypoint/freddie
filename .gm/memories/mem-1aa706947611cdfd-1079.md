freddie client perf: when input lag scales with SESSION HISTORY rather than with visible content, the cause is per-history-item work running on every render, not a leak or a slow function. Two confirmed instances: (1) ToolRow built every collapsed row's card body (ReadBlock/CodeBlock -> shiki TextMate tokenization) on every render regardless of open state; (2) ProducedFiles re-ran a write-then-read-width probe loop (forced reflow per candidate label) on every render regardless of whether its paths changed. Both fixed by gating the expensive work on the state that actually determines it (#everOpened latch; a pathsKey identity check). Method that found it: CDP performance_start_trace with reload:false + a BOTTOM-UP self-time aggregation over ProfileChunk samples (group by callFrame, weight by consecutive sample time deltas) -- the trace summary's own insights showed only a trivial ForcedReflow and hid a 33927ms shiki frame that the bottom-up view surfaced immediately. Prefer bottom-up self-time over the insight summary when a cost is spread across many small calls.
